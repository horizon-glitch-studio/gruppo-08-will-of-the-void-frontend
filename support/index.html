Support

Answers to the most common questions. If yours is not here, write to us.

Account

Why should I verify my email?

You need a verified email to propose or accept a trade. It also counts for the invite bonus of the Summoner who invited you.

Can I change my time zone?

Yes, once every 30 days. The daily bonus follows the day of your time zone.

How do I delete my account?

From your account settings, or by writing to support@willofthevoid.com. Once confirmed, your Ship, collection, decks, Trophies and unspent Shards are deleted for good and cannot be recovered. See the Privacy Policy.

Game

Do I make choices during a match?

No. The server plays the whole match from your deck, round by round. You choose the deck, and you can watch the replay.

Why is my deck not valid?

A deck stops being valid when it breaks a deck rule: for example after you trade one of its cards, or when a Wanderer in it leaves. Fix it or choose another deck. The rules are in the rulebook.

What is a defense deck?

The deck the game uses when another Summoner attacks you in Ranked. Without a valid one you cannot play Ranked.

Why can I not attack this Summoner again?

In Ranked you can attack the same Summoner once every 24 hours, in Quick 3 times. A Summoner with an active shield cannot be attacked in Ranked.

Shards and packs

How do I earn Shards?

By playing: a daily bonus, a bonus at registration, a comeback bonus after 7 days away, a reward at the end of each season you play Ranked, a reward the first time in a season you reach a higher Orbit, and a bonus when a Summoner you invited has verified their email and played 10 matches against different opponents. The amounts are in the rulebook.

Do I need to pay to play?

Every pack can be opened with Shards you earn by playing. For the first year Shards can only be earned. After that you will also be able to buy them.

What are the chances of finding a rare card?

Every slot of a pack has its own probabilities, listed in the rulebook. Guarantees stop long streaks of bad luck.

Where did my Wanderer go?

A Wanderer stays on board for 7 days, then moves to the memory archive: you can still see it, you cannot play it. If you find it again in a pack, it comes back.

Trades

Who can I trade with?

With any Summoner, friend or not. Both need a verified email.

Why did my trade fail?

Cards are checked when the trade is accepted. It fails if a card is no longer in the collection, or if one of the two Summoners would be left with fewer than 30 cards.
