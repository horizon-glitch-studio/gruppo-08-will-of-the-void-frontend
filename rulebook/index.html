The game in five lines

  1. You collect cards. Every card is a creature.
  2. You build a deck of 30 cards.
  3. You challenge another Summoner. The server plays the match: you make no moves, only your deck counts.
  4. Winning Ranked matches earns Trophies and moves you up the leaderboard.
  5. With Shards you open packs and find new cards.

Cards and rarities

  • Every card has a name, a faction, a rarity, an ATK and a DEF.
  • ATK is the damage a creature deals in every round. DEF is the damage it can take. Both are at least 1.
  • The rarer the card, the stronger it is. Rarity is the only measure of strength.

Wanderer cards

  • A Wanderer stays on board for 7 days from the moment you find it. The time left is always visible on the card.
  • You can own one copy of each Wanderer.
  • If you find a Wanderer that is still on board, it stays 7 more days. If it had already left, it comes back for 7 days.
  • When its time ends, the card moves to the memory archive with the date it left: you can still see it, you cannot play it.
  • Decks that hold it are not valid until you fix them. Matches already played are not affected.
  • Wanderer cards cannot be traded.

Factions

  • There are eight factions: six elemental (Lava, Electro, Radio, Ice, Metal, Matte) and two inert (Gem, Noble Gas).
  • Every elemental faction prevails over two factions, is overpowered by two and is even with one.
  • A creature facing a faction it prevails over gets +3 ATK for the whole clash. The other creature loses nothing.
  • Advantages never change DEF.
  • Gem and Noble Gas never give or receive an advantage.
  • No faction has more ATK or more DEF than the others: they differ only in their advantages.

Decks

  • A deck has exactly 30 cards.
  • At most 15 cards of the same elemental faction, so every deck mixes at least two factions.
  • You can use only cards you own and can play, in no more copies than you own.
  • You can save up to 20 decks.

At registration every Summoner receives the same starter deck, with cards of all six elemental factions and no Wanderer card. Card list: Coming soon

Defense deck

You can choose one of your decks as defense deck, and change it at any moment. The game uses it when another Summoner attacks you in Ranked. Without a valid defense deck you can neither attack nor be attacked in Ranked.

Match

The server plays the whole match: you make no choices during it, the strategy is all in your deck.

  1. Both Summoners start with 0 Energy.
  2. The server shuffles both decks. Nobody can influence the order.
  3. Each Summoner deploys the first card of their deck in the Arena.
  4. The two creatures clash in rounds. In every round they hit at the same time: each loses DEF equal to the effective ATK of the other, the ATK after the faction advantage.
  5. Lost DEF never comes back. ATK never changes. A creature with DEF 0 or less is defeated.
  6. If both are still alive, another round is played. The clash ends when at least one is defeated.

Energy

  • If only one creature is defeated, the other Summoner gains Energy equal to how far its DEF went below 0. At exactly 0, no Energy.
  • If both are defeated, nobody gains Energy.
  • The survivor stays in the Arena with the DEF it has left. The Summoner who lost a creature deploys the next card. If both were defeated, both Summoners deploy their next card.

How a match ends

  • The first Summoner to reach the win threshold wins: 30 Energy in Ranked and Friend, 20 in Quick.
  • If a Summoner has to deploy a card but has none left, the match ends. Each Summoner gains 4 Energy for every card they still have, the one in the Arena included. More Energy wins, equal Energy is a draw.
  • There is no turn limit.
  • The whole match is saved: both Summoners can watch the replay for 90 days. The result stays in the history.

Game modes

Mode Opponent Win threshold Trophies
Friend One of your friends 30 Energy No
Quick From a list of random Summoners 20 Energy No
Ranked From a list of Summoners with similar Trophies 30 Energy Yes
  • Matches are not played in real time: the server plays them at once.
  • Friend and Quick matches give no Trophies and no rewards.
  • In Friend and Quick, if the opponent has a valid defense deck, the match is played at once against it. Otherwise it becomes a challenge: the opponent can accept, choosing a deck, or reject. A challenge expires after 48 hours.
  • Ranked matches are always played at once.
  • In Quick and Ranked the game offers 5 opponents. You can attack each of them once per list.
  • You can attack the same Summoner once every 24 hours in Ranked, 3 times in Quick.
  • Shield: after 5 Ranked attacks received in 24 hours, nobody can attack you in Ranked for 12 hours. If you attack in Ranked, the shield ends.
  • After a Friend or Quick match you can ask for a rematch. Not in Ranked.

Ranking

  • You start with 0 Trophies, on the event horizon. Only Ranked matches change them.
  • Beating a Summoner with more Trophies gives more, beating one with fewer gives less. A draw counts as half a win. The loser loses what the winner gains.
  • Trophies never go below 0.
  • A season lasts 4 weeks. At the end, Trophies above 1000 are halved: from 1400 you restart at 1200.
  • At the end of a season, every Summoner who played at least one Ranked match receives Shards based on their Orbit.
  • The first time in a season you reach a higher Orbit, you receive Shards.
Orbit Name Trophies Season reward
1 Coming soon 0–199 1 × daily bonus
2 Coming soon 200–499 3 × daily bonus
3 Coming soon 500–999 6 × daily bonus
4 Coming soon 1000 and more 10 × daily bonus

The leaderboard is global and per Orbit. Only Summoners who played Ranked in the current season appear in it.

Packs

  • Packs are bought in the Shop for 300 Shards.
  • The standard pack is always on sale. It never holds Wanderer cards.
  • An event pack is on sale only during its event, and can hold the Wanderer cards of that event.
  • Every pack holds 5 cards, one per slot, and at least one card of Soldier rarity or higher.

Guarantees

  • Champion or rarer: after 9 packs in a row without one, the 10th holds one.
  • Elect: after 49 standard packs in a row without one, the 50th holds one.
  • Wanderer: after 49 event packs in a row without one, the 50th holds one.
  • Each count is separate: standard packs count only for the Elect, event packs only for the Wanderer, every pack for the Champion.
  • The guaranteed card takes the place of slot 5. A pack always holds 5 cards.

Shards

Shards are the currency of the game. They are spent only on packs and cannot be traded. Every pack can be opened with Shards you earn by playing.

For the first year Shards can only be earned. After that you will also be able to buy them.

Trades

  • You can trade cards with any Summoner, friend or not.
  • Both Summoners need a verified email.
  • Only cards are traded. Shards and Wanderer cards are not.
  • Each side can hold any number of cards. One side can be empty: that is a gift.
  • The receiver can accept or reject. The sender can cancel. A proposal expires after 48 hours.
  • Cards are checked when the trade is accepted. If a card is no longer there, the trade fails.
  • All the cards move together or none does.
  • After a trade each Summoner must own at least 30 cards, not counting Wanderer cards.
  • After a trade your decks are checked again.
