Glossary

The words of Will of the Void, grouped by topic. The full rules are in the rulebook.

No term matches your search.

World

Umbra
The galaxy where the game takes place.
Void
The conscious black hole at the center of Umbra. It feeds on the Energy of battles.
Arena
The place inside the Void where creatures fight. Every match happens in the Arena.
Summoner
The player. A Summoner commands a Ship and sends its creatures to fight in the Arena.
Ship
The Summoner's base, in orbit around the Void. The creatures found in packs live on board.
Event horizon
The narrative place of a Summoner with 0 Trophies: on the edge of the Void, not swallowed by it. Nobody is ever swallowed.

Cards

Card
A creature. Every card in the game is a creature, with ATK, DEF, a faction and a rarity. "Card" and "creature" are the same thing.
ATK
The damage a creature deals to the opposing creature in each round. Always at least 1.
DEF
The damage a creature can take. It goes down with damage and never regenerates during a match.
Faction
The element a creature is made of. It decides the advantages against other factions.
Rarity
The tier of a card. It decides its points and how many copies a deck can hold. The only measure of strength.
Collection
All the cards a Summoner owns, with their copies.
Memory archive
The part of the collection where departed Wanderer cards stay. Visible, not playable.

Factions

Lava
Elemental faction. Creatures of magma from planets close to the stars.
Electro
Elemental faction. Creatures of conductive materials and electromagnetic fields.
Radio
Elemental faction. Creatures of radioactive, unstable elements.
Ice
Elemental faction. Creatures of ice and frozen liquids from planets far from the stars.
Metal
Elemental faction. Creatures of metal, often magnetic.
Matte
Elemental faction. Creatures of dark carbon and opaque minerals.
Gem
Inert faction. Crystals formed in the core of planets. Every Gem card is an Elect card, and every Elect card is a Gem card.
Noble Gas
Inert faction. Creatures born in nebulae and between galaxies. Every Noble Gas card is a Wanderer card, and every Wanderer card is a Noble Gas card.
Elemental faction
Lava, Electro, Radio, Ice, Metal or Matte. Elemental factions give and receive advantages.
Inert faction
Gem or Noble Gas. An inert faction never gives or receives advantages.
Advantage
The modifier a faction gets against a faction it prevails over. It lasts for the whole clash.
Even
Two factions with no advantage between them. Two creatures of the same faction are always even.

Rarities

Captive
The most common rarity. Creatures freed by the Summoner in exchange for their help in the Arena.
Conscript
Common people who answered the call of the Ship.
Soldier
Trained troops sent by the armies of their worlds.
Champion
Heroes of their worlds who chose the Ship themselves.
Elect
The rarity of the Gem faction.
Wanderer
The rarest rarity, the rarity of the Noble Gas faction. A Wanderer stays on board for a limited time, then leaves. It cannot be traded.

Decks and packs

Deck
A set of cards from the collection, saved by the Summoner and used in a match. It must respect the deck constraints.
Valid deck
A deck that respects every deck constraint and uses only cards the Summoner owns and can play.
Starter deck
The deck every Summoner receives at registration. The same for everyone, with cards of every elemental faction.
Defense deck
The deck the system uses when other Summoners attack in Ranked. Required to play Ranked.
Shop
The section of the portal where Summoners buy packs with Shards.
Pack
A set of random cards bought with Shards. Opening a pack sends a call: new creatures answer and board the Ship.
Standard pack
The pack always on sale. It never contains Wanderer cards.
Event pack
A pack on sale for a limited period. It can contain Wanderer cards.
Slot
A position in a pack, with its own rarity probabilities.
Guarantee
A rule that grants a card of a given rarity after a number of packs without one.

Match

Match
A battle between two decks, simulated by the server from start to end without choices from the Summoners.
Clash
The fight between the two creatures in the Arena. It lasts one or more rounds and ends when at least one creature is defeated.
Round
One exchange inside a clash: both creatures hit each other at the same time.
Effective ATK
The ATK a creature uses in a clash, after the faction advantage.
Energy
The match score. A Summoner gains Energy when its creature defeats the opposing one. The first to reach the win threshold wins.
Win threshold
The Energy needed to win a match. It depends on the mode.
Deck exhaustion
The moment a deck has no cards left to deploy. The match ends.
End bonus
The Energy given for every card a Summoner still has, the card in the Arena included, when the match ends by deck exhaustion.
Draw
A match that ends with the same Energy for both Summoners.
Replay
The turn-by-turn record of a match, visible to both Summoners.

Modes

Friend match
A match against one of the Summoner's friends. No Trophies, no rewards.
Quick match
A match against a random, recently active Summoner. No Trophies, no rewards.
Ranked match
A match that changes Trophies. Both Summoners need a defense deck.
Challenge
A match request that the other Summoner must accept within a time limit. Used in Friend and Quick matches when the opponent has no defense deck.
Rematch
A new match between the same two Summoners right after a match. Friend and Quick only.
Opponent list
The opponents the system offers for a Ranked or Quick match, saved on the server.
Shield
A temporary protection from Ranked attacks, given after many attacks received. It ends if the Summoner attacks in Ranked.

Ranking

Trophies
The ranking score, changed only by Ranked matches. Never below 0.
Season
A ranking period. At the end of each season Trophies are partly reset.
Orbit
A ranking tier, set by Trophies. The higher the Orbit, the farther the Ship from the Void.
Leaderboard
The list of Summoners ordered by Trophies.

Economy and trades

Shards
The virtual currency. Summoners spend Shards on packs. In phase 1 Shards are only earned; buying them with real money comes in phase 2.
Phase 1
The launch period, without real-money purchases.
Phase 2
From month 13 after launch: Shards can be bought with real money.
Trade
An exchange of cards between two Summoners, in any quantity. It happens only if the receiver accepts.
