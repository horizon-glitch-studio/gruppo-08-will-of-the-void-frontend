The world

At the center of the galaxy of Umbra there is a black hole older than the stars. It thinks, it remembers, and it feeds on the Energy of battles.

Summoners command Ships in orbit around the Void. The creatures they call board the Ship and fight in the Arena, inside the Void. As long as they fight, their worlds stay whole.

Eight factions

Every creature is made of an element. A creature facing a faction it prevails over gets +3 ATK for the whole clash. Choose your faction and an opponent.

Climb away from the Void

Ranked matches win and lose Trophies. More Trophies place your Ship in a higher Orbit, farther from the Void.

Every Summoner starts with 0 Trophies, on the event horizon. Seasons last 4 weeks: at the end, every Summoner who played Ranked earns Shards based on their Orbit. Nobody is ever swallowed.

Questions

Do I make choices during a match?

No. The server plays the whole match from your deck, round by round. You choose the deck, and you can watch the replay.

Do I need to pay to play?

Every pack can be opened with Shards you earn by playing. For the first year Shards can only be earned.

Where do I play?

In the browser, on phone or computer, after signing in. There is nothing to download.
