  • Lava

    Planets close to the stars, covered in magma.

  • Electro

    Planets of conductive materials, wrapped in strong electromagnetic fields.

  • Radio

    Planets of heavy, unstable elements, subject to radioactive decay.

  • Ice

    Planets and comets far from the stars, where water and other common substances exist only as ice or freezing liquid.

  • Metal

    Metallic planets, often strongly magnetic.

  • Matte

    Dark planets of rock and carbon-based materials: opaque and insulating.

  • Gem

    Crystals formed in the core of planets.

  • Noble Gas

    Creatures born in nebulae and between galaxies.
